My Top 3 Favorite Movies

Little Women by Greta Gerwig

I love the book so much and the way Greta wrote her own interpretation is perfect and really adds up to the story instead of changing it.

Dead Poets Society

I just saw this movie a couple of days ago but It touched me the storytelling and the personalities of the main characters Neil, Todd and Charlie will have a special place in my heart forever.

Your Name

Easy my favorite movie ever, I have rewatched it so many times because it is just perfect, the red string theory the plot the pacing and the beautiful art
